● About

I work at the intersection of data analysis and business analysis — gathering requirements, cleaning and validating multi-source data, and documenting the processes that keep reporting solutions running. My background spans institutional research, program review, and accreditation reporting, and it always starts from the same question: is this finding solid enough to act on?

I work primarily in Python and SQL, applying regression, segmentation, and hypothesis testing to surface patterns that hold up under scrutiny. Then I translate them into Power BI and Tableau dashboards, SOPs, and documentation that let both technical and non-technical stakeholders act with confidence.
